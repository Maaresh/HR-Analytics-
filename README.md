# HR Analytics Dashboard | SQL, Excel & Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing HR employee data to understand workforce trends and employee attrition. The analysis uses **SQL, Excel, and Power BI** to explore key HR metrics and convert employee data into meaningful business insights.

The project examines factors such as **department, job role, salary, overtime, job satisfaction, work-life balance, experience, tenure, training, and employee attrition**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall employee attrition.
* Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
* Understand the relationship between overtime and attrition.
* Analyze employee salary and compensation patterns.
* Evaluate job satisfaction and work-life balance.
* Analyze employee experience and tenure.
* Identify workforce trends that can support HR decision-making.
* Present findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### Employee Attrition

Analyzes employees who left the organization and compares attrition across different workforce dimensions.

### Department & Job Role

Examines employee distribution and attrition across departments and job roles.

### Salary Analysis

Analyzes monthly income patterns and employee distribution across different salary groups.

### Overtime Analysis

Examines the relationship between employee overtime and attrition.

### Employee Satisfaction

Analyzes job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, and work-life balance in relation to employee attrition.

### Employee Experience

Analyzes total working years, years at the company, years in the current role, and years since the last promotion.
